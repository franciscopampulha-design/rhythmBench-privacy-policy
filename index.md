# Privacy Policy — Rhythm Bench

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Rhythm Bench ("the app") is made by Francisco Ferreira Pampulha, who is
responsible for the data described here. Questions or requests:
franciscopampulha@gmail.com.

## The short version

- Your practice history, settings and progress stay **on your device**.
  There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.
- With your permission, the app sends **usage statistics** to Google
  Analytics for Firebase to help improve it. In Europe nothing is sent
  unless you agree; everywhere else you can turn it off at any time.
- The app has **no ads**, does not collect your advertising identifier and
  does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your practice history, scores, settings, saved rhythms and
audio-latency calibration in the app's own storage on your device. It is
never uploaded by the app. If you use the export feature, you choose where
the exported file goes. Deleting the app deletes this data, subject to your
device's own backups.

## Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

**What is collected, when statistics are on:**

- which screens are opened, and when exercise sessions start and finish
  (exercise type, number of questions, how many were correct);
- whether the Premium upgrade was viewed or bought (product and price, as
  reported by Google Analytics);
- device and app information such as device model, operating system, app
  version and language;
- a random app-instance identifier created by Firebase;
- an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP
  address. Google does not store the full IP address.

**What is never collected:** your name, email address or any account,
your taps or recordings, the contents of your practice history, your
advertising identifier, or precise location.

**Why:** to understand which parts of the app are used and where it can be
improved. It is never used for advertising or sold.

**Your choice:**

- **In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the
  rest of Europe**, the app asks you first and sends nothing unless you
  choose "Share statistics". The legal basis is your consent (Article
  6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Elsewhere**, statistics are on by default and you can turn them off.
- To change your choice at any time: open the menu (⋮) on any tab →
  **Usage statistics**. Turning it off stops collection and resets the
  analytics identifier on your device.

**Who processes it:** Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, as a service
provider (processor) for the app. Data may be processed outside your
country, including in the United States, under Google's safeguards such as
the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. See
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**How long:** Google Analytics keeps event-level data for the retention
period set in the app's Analytics settings (at most 14 months), after
which it is deleted. Aggregated reports do not identify anyone.

## Purchases

Premium is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store
handles the payment, and the app never sees your payment details. The app
only learns whether Premium is unlocked, and keeps that on your device.

## Children

The app is a general-audience music training tool. It does not knowingly
collect personal information from children, and it collects no data that
identifies anyone. In Europe, statistics are only sent with consent.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR),
you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object
to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw
consent at any time. Because the app has no account and analytics data is
not linked to your identity, the quickest way to exercise these rights is
in the app itself: turn off **Usage statistics**, which also resets your
analytics identifier, or delete the app to remove all local data. You can
also write to franciscopampulha@gmail.com, and you have the right to
complain to your local data protection authority.
