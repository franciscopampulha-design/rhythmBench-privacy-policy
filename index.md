❯ what is the advertising identifier, or precise location.

These are two kinds of personal data that many apps collect and Rhythm Bench doesn't. The sentence lists them to make that clear.

Advertising identifier. This is an ID number that your phone gives to apps for advertising: IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android. It's the same number in every app, so ad companies use it to recognise you across apps and show targeted   ads. Rhythm Bench turns its Analytics never receives it.
Precise location. This is where you are exactly, from GPS, to within a few metres. Apps need your permission for it, which is the "Allow location?" prompt. Rhythm Bench never asks for location. The only location Google sees is a rough country or region, worked out from youraddress, and the policy saysso above.

So the sentence means the app doesn't collect anything that could follow you around the internet or show where you are.
